Bayline support

Bayline is a dock puzzle by Lantern Bay Games. Tap a stack to move its front crate into the hold. Three crates of the same color ship out. Fill the hold before they match and the dock jams.

Contact

Email lanternbaygames@gmail.com with the level number and your device. We usually reply within two days. You can also use Send feedback on the home screen.

Common questions

Is every level solvable? Yes. Every board is checked by a solver before it ships.

I jammed. What now? Use undo, or start the level again. A new rule arrives every 10 levels; the help button on the board explains it.

Where is my progress saved? On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
