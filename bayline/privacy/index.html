Bayline privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Bayline is a free dock puzzle for Android and for iPhone and iPad (iOS). This page describes what version 1.2.0 of the game does with information on each platform. Where Android and iOS differ, the section says so.

Progress on your device

The game stores progress on the device: the campaign level, how many crates have shipped, best moves, the daily streak, daily free-use flags for Undo and Add a berth, bought continues, and whether sound is muted. That data stays in the app's local storage. Bayline has no account and no server of its own, and this progress is not uploaded to the developer. On Android, if you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy of this progress is also kept in Google Play Games Saved Games (see below). iOS has no cloud save.

Google Play Games (Android only)

Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. The game can be played without signing in. If you sign in, Bayline unlocks Play Games achievements, submits your best daily streak to a Play Games leaderboard, and saves a copy of your progress to Play Games Saved Games so it can be restored on another Android device. Google handles that sign-in and stores that data under Google's policies. Bayline does not create its own account from that sign-in. The iOS version has no Play Games or Game Center sign-in.

Notifications (Android only)

On Android, Bayline can schedule reminders on the device. Those notifications are created locally. The game does not send a device token or the reminder schedule to a developer server. The iOS version does not schedule notifications.

In-app review (Android only)

On Android, Bayline can ask Google Play to show its in-app review sheet. Whether that sheet appears is up to Google Play. Bayline does not receive the rating.

Advertising

On Android and iOS, Bayline shows ads using Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). Ads start at level 9, never in the tutorial. A banner sits on the home screen. An interstitial ad can appear between some levels. A rewarded ad plays only when you choose an option labelled "watch an ad", for an extra Undo or Add a berth after the free daily uses of those actions. Buying Remove ads turns these ads off.

AdMob may collect and share device identifiers (on Android, the Advertising ID; on iOS, the identifier for vendor and, only if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction, performance, crash, and other diagnostic data. Google uses that information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google describes this in its policy for partner sites and its privacy policy.

Tracking on iPhone and iPad (App Tracking Transparency)

After the first level, iOS asks whether Bayline may track you. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized with it. The game works the same either way. You can change this at any time in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Consent

In regions that require it (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), a Google UMP consent form is shown before ads, on Android and iOS. Players in those regions can reopen their choices from Privacy options on the Bayline home screen. On Android, you can also reset or delete the Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Android settings: Settings, then Google, then Ads. On iOS, use the Tracking setting described above.

In-app purchases

The Shop sells Remove ads and packs of continues. Purchases are made through Apple's App Store on iOS and Google Play on Android. Bayline uses RevenueCat to process and check them. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt and an anonymous app user ID created on your device. It does not get your name or email from Bayline. Bayline never sees your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Feedback email

Send feedback opens your own email app with a new message to the developer, with the subject "Bayline feedback". Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you do, the developer receives your email address and what you write, and uses them only to reply and to improve the game.

What this version does not do

Bayline does not create an account and has no server of its own. It does not sell personal information apart from the advertising sharing with Google described above.

Children

Bayline is a puzzle for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13.

Removing data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes the progress stored on the device, including bought continues. A Remove ads purchase stays with your Apple ID or Google account and can be restored with Restore purchases in the Shop. On Android, progress saved to Play Games Saved Games is held in your Google account and can be removed through Google Play Games.
