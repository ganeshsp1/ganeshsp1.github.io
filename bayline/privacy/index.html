Bayline privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Bayline is a free dock puzzle for Android. This page describes what this version of the game does with information.

Progress on your device

The game stores progress on the device only: the campaign level, how many crates have shipped, best moves, the daily streak, and whether sound is muted. That data stays in the app's local storage. Bayline has no account and no server of its own, and this progress is not uploaded to the developer.

Notifications

Bayline can schedule reminders on the device. Those notifications are created locally. The game does not send a device token or the reminder schedule to a developer server.

Google Play Games

Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. The game can be played without signing in. In this version, achievement and leaderboard ids are placeholders, so the game does not submit achievement unlocks or scores. If you choose to sign in, Google handles that sign-in under Google's policies. Bayline does not create its own account from that sign-in.

In-app review

Bayline can ask Google Play to show its in-app review sheet. Whether that sheet appears is up to Google Play. Bayline does not receive the rating.

Advertising

Bayline shows ads using Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). A rewarded ad plays only when you choose it, for an extra Undo or Add a berth after the free daily uses of those actions. An interstitial ad can appear between some levels. A banner sits on the home screen.

AdMob may collect and share device identifiers (including the Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction and diagnostic data. Google uses that information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google describes this in its policy for partner sites and its privacy policy.

Consent

In regions that require it (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), a Google UMP consent form is shown before ads. Players in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland can reopen choices from Privacy options on the Bayline home screen. You can also change your choices by resetting the Advertising ID, or by opting out of ads personalization, in Android settings: Settings, then Google, then Ads. From that screen you can delete or reset the Advertising ID.

What this version does not do

Bayline does not create an account. It does not sell personal information apart from the advertising sharing with Google described above.

Children

Bayline is a puzzle for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13.

Removing data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes the progress stored on the device.
